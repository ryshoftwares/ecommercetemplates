From selection to launch

The same five steps apply to every template in this library.

1
Browse

Open a template's live demo and click through Home, Shop, Product, Cart, and mobile views.

2
Choose a platform

Every design is available as a WordPress/WooCommerce theme or a Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme.

3
Install

Upload the matching ZIP from /PACKAGES — Appearance → Themes on WordPress, or Online Store → Themes on Shopify.

4
Customize

Add the client's logo, products, images, and colors via the WordPress Customizer or the Shopify Theme Editor.

5
Launch

Connect a domain and payment gateway, and the store is live.

See MASTER-README.md for full installation and customization documentation.
