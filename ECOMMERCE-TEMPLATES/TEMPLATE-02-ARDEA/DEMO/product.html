Most Ardea pieces are made to order and ship within 2–4 weeks. Small items (lighting, decor, textiles) are sent by standard courier in protective packaging. Chairs, tables and case goods ship via our furniture delivery partner with room-of-choice placement; sofas, beds, wardrobes and kitchen islands include two-person white-glove delivery with packaging removal.

Where assembly is required, the product page and included printed guide will say so explicitly — this chair ships fully assembled with no tools needed. Delivery windows are confirmed by SMS and email once your order leaves the workshop.

Dust the oak frame weekly with a dry microfibre cloth; re-oil every 12–18 months with food-safe furniture oil. Spot clean linen with a damp cloth and mild soap; covers may be dry cleaned. Keep out of direct, prolonged sunlight to avoid uneven fading.
