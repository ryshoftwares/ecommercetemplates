Our Story

Furniture built to outlast the trend cycle

Ardea started in 2016 with a single question: why does most furniture get worse, not better, with age? Everything we design since has been an answer — solid wood over veneer, natural fibre over synthetic blends, and joinery that can be repaired instead of replaced.

What We Believe

Three ideas guide every piece we make

Solid materials, honestly used

No veneer over MDF, no particleboard cores. When we say oak, walnut or marble, that's what's inside the piece — visible in every cut edge and drawer joint.

Made slowly, on purpose

Most pieces take 2–4 weeks to build to order. We'd rather you wait a little longer for something finished properly than receive it faster and worse.

Designed to be repaired

Bolted rather than glued joints, replaceable cushion covers, and a 15-year structural warranty — because the most sustainable piece of furniture is the one you keep.

How We Got Here

A short history of Ardea

2016

A workshop in Bengaluru

Ardea began with two founders and one workshop partner, making dining chairs to order for a handful of Bengaluru clients.

2019

The Oak Lounge Chair

After five years of prototyping, our signature steam-bent lounge chair went into full production and became our best-selling piece.

2022

Twelve workshop partners

We expanded into lighting, kitchen and decor, partnering with small workshops across four states rather than opening a single large factory.

2025

15-year structural warranty

Confident in our joinery, we extended our structural warranty to 15 years across the full furniture range.

Our Craft

Twelve workshops, not one factory

Every Ardea piece passes through the hands of fewer than twelve people, from timber yard to your living room. We work with family-run workshops in Karnataka, Rajasthan, Punjab and Kerala — each chosen for a specific craft: oak joinery, cane weaving, stone finishing or natural-dye textiles.

This means slower production and smaller batches than a factory model — but also means a broken drawer runner or a loosened cane panel can actually be repaired, by people who understand how it was built in the first place.
