The Oak Lounge Chair

One chair, five years of prototypes

Our founding designer spent five years refining a single steam-bent oak frame before it ever went into production — testing joinery, seat pitch and linen weight until the chair felt as good after a decade of use as it did on day one.

Every Oak Lounge Chair is built from FSC-certified European oak, upholstered in undyed heavyweight linen over a webbed jute seat, and finished entirely by hand in our partner workshop.
