The Velora Story

Considered clothing, made in small batches, built to outlast the season it was designed for.

Since 2014

Two people, one sewing table, and a refusal to compromise on fabric

Velora began in a single room above a fabric wholesaler in Bengaluru, where our founder Anaïs Moreau and a pattern-cutter friend produced their first run of twelve silk dresses. The brief hasn't changed since: use real materials, cut with real tailoring, and sell fewer, better pieces rather than chasing every trend cycle.

Twelve years on, Velora works with a small number of European mills for wool and cashmere, and Indian ateliers for cutting, sewing and finishing — keeping production close to where our customers are, and keeping our supply chain small enough to actually know by name.

What We Stand For

Three commitments behind every piece

Made to Last

Every pattern is tested for fit across three sample rounds before production, and finished with reinforced seams designed to survive years, not one season.

Responsibly Sourced

92% of our fabrics are natural or recycled fibres, traced back to mills we've personally audited for labour and environmental standards.

Small-Batch Production

We produce in limited runs to reduce overproduction and waste — when a style sells out, it may not be restocked, by design rather than accident.
