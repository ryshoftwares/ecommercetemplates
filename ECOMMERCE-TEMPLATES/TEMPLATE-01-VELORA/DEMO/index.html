Our Story

Crafted in small batches, built to last seasons

Founded in 2014, Velora began as a two-person atelier focused on a single idea: clothing should be made from real materials, cut with real tailoring, and sold without the churn of fast fashion. Today we work with a small number of European mills and Indian ateliers to produce considered collections twice a year.
