Our Story

Rituals, Not Routines

NOAÉ began in 2021 in a rented Mumbai apartment, where our founder — tired of ten-step routines that promised everything and delivered very little — started mixing small batches of serum on her kitchen counter. She wasn't chasing trends. She wanted three honest products that did exactly what the label said, in a bottle whose ingredient list was short enough to actually read out loud.

What began as jars handed to friends became NOAÉ: a beauty label built on the belief that skin, hair and mind respond best to consistency, not complexity. Every formula spends months in dermatological review before it reaches a shelf, and every ingredient has to earn its place — nothing is added just to bulk up a bottle or pad an ingredient list.

Today NOAÉ ships across India from our formulation studio, but the premise hasn't changed. Fewer, better products. Ingredient-forward transparency. And rituals simple enough that you'll actually keep them tomorrow, and the day after that.

What We Stand For

Three Promises We Don't Break

These aren't marketing lines — they're the checklist every NOAÉ product has to pass before it's allowed to launch.

🧴

Ingredient Integrity

Every formula lists actives at their real, visible concentrations — never hidden behind a vague "proprietary blend."

📦

Considered Packaging

Glass jars, recyclable cartons and refill-ready bottles, chosen to be reused long before they're thrown away.

🌿

Real Results

We test for measurable outcomes — hydration, barrier repair, scalp health — not just how nice a formula feels going on.

How We Formulate

Fewer Ingredients, More Intention

Before a NOAÉ formula reaches the lab bench, it has to pass one question: does this ingredient change how the product performs, or is it just there for show? If the answer is the latter, it doesn't make the cut.

🔬

Batch-Tested

Each small batch is checked for stability and potency before bottling.

🩺

Dermatologist-Reviewed

Independent review across skin types, including sensitive and reactive skin.

🐇

Cruelty-Free, Always

No animal testing at any stage, from raw material to finished product.

🌸

Fragrance Layered Last

Scent is added only after performance is locked, never to mask an unfinished formula.
